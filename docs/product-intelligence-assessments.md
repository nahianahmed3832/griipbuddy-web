# Product intelligence assessments and analyst

Assessments use the selected operating market, reporting calendar, date range, and evidence scope. Each entry gives its sample, factual observations, limitations, and a distinct hypothesis. Confidence labels describe the amount and quality of supporting evidence. They are qualitative review labels.

| Assessment | Evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| Demonstrated demand | At least five verified completed orders and five units. Completion requires an authoritative capture or COD collection. Positive contribution is shown only with complete current cost evidence. |
| Growing demand | Two adjacent equal windows of complete local weeks, at least 30 visible impressions and at least 75% available stock coverage in each. The same weekdays occur in both windows. Both windows must have the same dominant campaign and placement with shares within 25 percentage points and discount shares within 20 percentage points. Require at least three additional completed units, 25% growth in completed units relative to the prior window, and 20% growth in paid units per impression. Holidays, attribution gaps, and missing inventory observations can limit growth comparisons. |
| Unmet search | At least three distinct measured searchers, three zero-result observations, and a zero share of at least 50%. Reformulations are observed nearby queries within the same session. |
| Possible variant gap | Apply the unmet-search rule, then look for an unambiguous, multiword eligible product name in the query with one to three additional tokens absent from the current variant names. Validate attributes and search aliases before treating the name match as an assortment gap. |
| Assortment test | Six distinct searchers and a 75% zero-result share, with no possible variant match. This is interest evidence for a trial. Use verified purchases and needed costs to assess the test. |
| Conversion | At least 30 visible impressions and 30 unique viewing journeys, no observed stock or delivery constraint, and two eligible peers in the same department with comparable campaign, placement, and discount mix. The linked view-to-cart rate must be at least five points below and at most 70% of the peer median. A Wilson interval describes approximate sampling uncertainty for the measured share. |
| Availability | Show only documented stockout or partially available observation days, zero current available stock, or disabled market delivery coverage. Require at least 10 viewing journeys, three cart sessions, or three verified completed orders. Check checkout for individual address coverage. |
| Weak contribution | Positive verified collections and zero or negative contribution supported by at least three orders with complete current cost inputs. Mixed-order costs require a verified allocation. |

Exposure and availability adjustments use observable measures. View-to-cart, view-to-checkout and view-to-payment are each linked independently to an earlier measured product view. Any seasonal, price, delivery or placement explanation requires its own test. An offsite advertising feed, supplier data, address-level delivery history and long-run search history are separate prerequisites for more granular analysis.

## Analyst connection

The staff analyst classifies a question locally, reads the authorized report, and links to the supporting local board or product view. Calculations, evidence scope, URLs, and any proposed quantities come from the deterministic report and purchasing rules. If a model adds an explanation, staff see it as a proposed interpretation with a source link. Drafting remains a separate staff action.

To enable the optional explanation provider in an authorized runtime, set `PRODUCT_INTELLIGENCE_ANALYST_PROVIDER=openai`, a structured-output model in `PRODUCT_INTELLIGENCE_ANALYST_MODEL`, and the authorized key in the runtime secret `PRODUCT_INTELLIGENCE_ANALYST_API_KEY`. The request uses the fixed Responses API endpoint, a four-second timeout, and `store: false`. It sends the market, dates, dataset class, intent category, approved evidence identifiers, and aggregate samples. Search terms, staff question text, product and supplier content, record links, and customer identifiers stay in the application. The evidence mode remains available when the credential, provider, response or evidence is unavailable. [Responses API reference](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/resources/responses/methods/create)

Marketplace development requires `PRODUCT_INTELLIGENCE_ANALYST_MAX_CALLS_PER_24H` from 1 to 1,000 and allows provider calls for the test dataset. The first provider attempt starts a 24-hour quota window. The quota is shared across both markets and all staff using the development database. Attempts consume quota before contacting the provider, and the deterministic answer remains available once the cap is reached. Set provider-side spending controls on the dedicated development project separately.
