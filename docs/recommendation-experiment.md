# Homepage recommendations experiment

The `homepage-recommendations-v1` plan compares the existing editorial Featured picks with the recommendation service in the same homepage slot. The treatment uses permitted shopping history when available, then contextual recommendations or the existing editorial fallback. Other placements follow their merchandising configuration. The staging cohort demonstrates tracking and reconciliation with fixture and sandbox outcomes.

## Enrollment and privacy

- Enrollment requires the isolated market-review database, local test database, or the dedicated Marketplace development acceptance profile, plus `ACQUISITION_MEASUREMENT=1` and `GRIIP_RECOMMENDATION_EXPERIMENT=1`. The storefront must have an enabled recommended placement, market behavior enabled, and a server-verified Featured picks baseline for the same market, locale, and current policy. The production runtime cannot enroll through this plan.
- Eligible journeys have an active signed shopping measurement choice and current personalization permission. Global Privacy Control and staff sessions suppress enrollment. Replay has its own choice.
- The randomization unit is the consented browser journey in the selected market. The server assigns 50/50 by a deterministic hash. Signing in during the journey preserves the arm. Switching markets uses the other market's independent assignment, and returning to the original market restores its assignment. Different browsers or expired journeys can enroll independently.
- The service records assignment before choosing the ranking. The browser records exposure only after a card in the assigned slot passes the existing foreground visibility check. The collector verifies the assignment against the current market, journey and consent generation. It stores one first exposure per assignment and placement. Impressions and clicks carry the server-verified assignment reference and recommendation version.
- Disabling or resetting personalization deletes the assignment and its exposure and removes experiment metadata on ordinary shopping events. Withdrawing shopping measurement deletes the journey and its measurement links. Saved orders and payments remain. The report is limited to the retained cohorts and can change when a shopper withdraws consent.

## Analysis registered for version 1

- Analyze by originally assigned arm within each market. Include all assigned journeys even if the slot never becomes visible. Count visible exposure separately as a check on delivery of the treatment.
- The primary metric is the share of assigned mature journeys with at least one verified prepaid capture or COD collection from an attributable order. Eligible orders are booked after assignment and within seven days or the signed measurement journey expiry, whichever is earlier. The paid or collected outcome must arrive by day 14 after assignment. A booked COD order, a delivered order, and a carrier remittance each provide different evidence.
- Collected revenue per eligible visitor uses gross verified captures and COD cash collections through day 14 divided by all mature assigned journeys in that arm. Refunds and fees enter contribution evidence when available. Each market uses its original currency.
- Use a fixed 21-day follow-up for deliveries, COD remittance, and received returns. Delivery uses all attributed orders in the 21-day mature cohort as the denominator. The return rate uses delivered orders. Contribution uses the existing order-level reconciled financial evidence and is unavailable if required inputs are missing. Imported bridge orders currently lack this cost evidence.
- Guardrails show the p95 server time on the first assigned recommendation response and the share of checkout journeys with an observed error. The results include verified provider failure evidence when available. Checkout event delivery is asynchronous and the figures reflect observed events.
- Show both arm sample sizes, pending windows, approximate 95% Wilson rate intervals, and a conservative difference range for purchase conversion. Interpret a cohort only after each arm has at least 1,000 mature journeys and 20 completed purchases. Review allocation imbalance and mixed homepage or ranking versions. The report does not select a winner. These are review thresholds, and a production launch would need a market-specific power and calendar plan.
- Use a new experiment plan ID if the eligible population, allocation, treatment, baseline, or analysis changes. Keep the homepage and market recommendation configuration stable within any cohort used for decisions.
