# Demand forecasts and purchasing proposals

Product Intelligence forecasts booked units in each market's local calendar. A booking is a saved native order or a verified legacy booking with a mapped product or variant. Payments, fulfilled units, cancellations, and returns remain distinct evidence. Booked units are suitable for estimating stock demand at booking time. They do not estimate retained sales or imply a verified collection.

## Baselines and evidence

The recent-mean baseline uses the last 56 days. It needs 21 known available and unpromoted days, at least ten of those in the last 28 days, and at least four positive days. A seasonal naive baseline repeats the most recently observed usable weekday in the last three weeks. The intermittent Croston–SBA baseline uses eligible intervals and nonzero arrivals with a smoothing factor of 0.2. It is compared when at most 60% of usable days have demand. Forecast horizons range from one to 56 days. The staff adapter uses at most 180 days of the selected market's available history and excludes the incomplete local day.

Stockouts, partial availability, unknown stock, active promotions, and unknown promotion coverage are censored in both fitting and evaluation. A known unpromoted day with eligible stock and zero bookings remains a zero. Units returned in the observed window are reported separately. The baseline predicts ordinary unpromoted bookings. It does not estimate lost sales during a stockout, incremental campaign demand, or net retained units.

Rolling holdouts use disjoint windows matching the horizon. Each prediction trains exclusively on dates before its holdout. An initial 60% of the usable windows may choose which baseline to surface. The comparison must show at least a 10% reduction in mean absolute error on at least four matched windows or keep the recent-mean baseline. The results table reports the later windows, average absolute error in units, signed error (positive means overprediction), WAPE when actual units are positive, and observed interval coverage when there are at least five evaluated ranges. With fewer than eight total rolling windows, all are shown for inspection and no model is selected from them.

The displayed range uses asymmetric, horizon-matched historical residuals from at least eight eligible windows. Intervals in backtests are computed from still-earlier residuals. The nominal level and scored coverage are reported separately. Sparse or newly available series may provide a baseline point without an estimated range. The summary compares series by market, product group, and product or variant level. A group rollup is descriptive and weighted by its scored windows. The board shows at most six series per market or product selection and discloses omitted rows.

Optional `GRIIP_INTELLIGENCE_ANALOGUES` points to explicitly reviewed same-market variants of the same item. Its JSON array entries have `marketId`, `productId`, `variantId`, `sourceVariantId`, `rationale`, `unitsRatio`, and `ratioRange` (a lower and upper ratio). A source must currently be eligible for the market and have a native fitted range at the same date and horizon. The ratio and reviewed range must be between 0.25 and 4. The resulting scenario range is an assumption about comparable demand and has no validated coverage for the new variant. When an analogue is unavailable, the report shows insufficient evidence.

## Purchasing inputs

Set `GRIIP_INTELLIGENCE_PURCHASING` only when an authorized operator can supply dated, reconciled records. The JSON array accepts one entry per physical pool and variant with `poolId`, `variantId`, `markets`, `currency`, `source`, `asOfDay`, `leadTimeDays`, `reviewDays`, `supplierOnTimeRate`, `safetyStockUnits`, `serviceTarget`, `minimumOrderUnits`, `unitsPerCase`, `shelfLifeDays`, `unitCostMinor`, `availableBudgetMinor`, `reservedUnits`, and `incoming`. Each incoming item has a unique `id`, positive `units`, and `expectedOn` local date. Use explicit `null` for a nonperishable item or a budget that does not apply. Use explicit recorded zero reservations or an empty inbound ledger where verified. Unconfigured purchasing data leaves those fields unavailable. Refresh this dated position at least every seven days and reconcile overdue arrivals. Costs use the selected local procurement currency in minor units. There is no automatic purchase or supplier request.

Replenishment compares a calibrated forecast at lead time plus review cadence against available stock, confirmed receipts, and safety stock. The service does not subtract reservations again from checkout's free inventory. The proposal rounds up to case size and minimum order, then checks usable shelf life and any purchasing budget. Overdue or unreliable receipts, mismatched inventory, unavailable forecasts, and incomplete inputs suppress quantity. A shared pool requires a single authorized combined review and forecasts for every consuming market. The single-market product board directs staff to review shared stock as one pool. Models do not sum purchasing currencies.

Forecasts are evaluated on read against the recorded daily facts. The inventory observation job supplies availability and price history. Keep its market profile and production/test classification consistent. Operational supplier reliability, reservations, incoming receipts, cost and budget inputs need a verified source or operator records before action. Use labelled fixtures for review, and use the staff interface to save draft proposals separately from execution.
